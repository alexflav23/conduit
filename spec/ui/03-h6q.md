# 03 — Demand / H6Q (`h6q`)
Status: COVERED ✅ (refresh) · Roles: sales (capture), finance/ceo (board) · Backend: `GET /h6q/coverage|matrix|by-sku|reconcile`, `GET/POST /h6q/my-forecasts`

## Purpose
The deepest board (doc 20 D7/D8): the weekly bottom-up forecast **capture** (agents) and the **coverage board**
(market × period × scenario × group-by) where human + 12k model rows meet — the demand truth that drives supply.

## Layout
- `PageHead` "Demand (H6Q)" + a **Board / Capture** mode switch (two personas).
- **Capture:** my accounts + open cycle → submit (SKU-mix-aware, show the per-SKU split, don't hide it).
- **Coverage board:** grand total + by-branch matrix; **scenario toggle P50/P80/P20**; group-by market/branch/SKU.
- **Reconcile:** forecast vs shipped vs activated per agent — the accountability view.
- Model rows (`source='model'`) visually distinguished from human capture.

## Components
`PageHead`, mode switch, capture form, the dense `Coverage` matrix, scenario `Chip` toggle, model/human badges.

## Data & layers
Units are `volume`; any revenue projection is `commercial` (collapse). The board is the densest screen — "extremely
readable, extremely functional."

## Actions & states
Submit forecast (capture) · toggle scenario/group-by (board). *Empty:* "no open cycle." Capture optimised for
weekly repeat speed.

## Design notes
Two personas, one domain — capture must be fast (weekly ceremony); the board must be dense yet legible. Model vs
human legibility is essential (the engine's rows must be honestly marked).
